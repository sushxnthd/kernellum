PHYSICALULX3S

Post-route timing enters architecture selection.

A 1/2/4/8-lane ULX3S-85F sweep shows that cycle-faster candidates can lose after physical timing: 2 lanes reaches 29.64 MHz and closes the 25 MHz target, while 4 and 8 lanes do not. CI generates the selected reference bitstream.
