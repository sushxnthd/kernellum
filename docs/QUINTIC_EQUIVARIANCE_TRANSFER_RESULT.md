# Quintic equivariance transfer: a predictive cross-family mechanism

## Decision

The frozen transfer gate survives.

KERNELLUM's exact pre-outcome diagnostic transported antipodal certificate
amortization from the coupled phi4 system to a disjoint coupled quintic family.
It also predicted the failure boundary: every symmetry-breaking forced variant
abstained before construction.

## Prospective failure prediction

Eight nonzero first-coordinate forcing variants have exact oddness defects from
(2/1000) through (16/1000). The diagnostic rejected all 8/8 before any
residual or Jacobian construction call. This rules out a selector that merely
routes every member of the new family.

## Project consequence

The selector is no longer phi4-specific. KERNELLUM may activate antipodal
certificate amortization for any registered family that supplies the complete
equivariance, orbit, existence, and uniqueness proof obligations. Missing or
broken obligations remain fail-closed.

## Limits and adversarial boundary

- The proof certifies one root per reported sign box, not global completeness
  outside their union.
- Only global sign symmetry is implemented; larger or non-abelian groups are
  not covered.
- The finite arithmetic proof obligations are trusted inputs to the runtime
  route and must be independently generated or verified.
- Approximate symmetry is intentionally rejected; no perturbative error bound
  is claimed.
- The calibration pilot is not prospective evidence and is retained separately.
