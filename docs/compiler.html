04 / Claim boundary

What the compiler is and is not.

Supported today

Dense graph subset to hardware IR to INT8 search to RTL.

  • Explicit operator validation
  • Generated weights and golden vectors
  • Cycle, RTL, synthesis and P&R evidence
Not claimed

Arbitrary graphs, production readiness or manufacturable silicon.

  • No arbitrary ONNX operator coverage
  • No physical-board latency or power result yet
  • No claim of ASIC readiness or production deployment
