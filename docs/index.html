Research direction

Kernellum investigates how AI workloads can be mapped automatically onto specialized hardware under explicit latency, memory, area and energy constraints. Public claims are tied to code, tests, synthesis evidence or measured hardware.

Efficient edge AI

03

AI for EDA

Explore automated graph lowering, architecture generation, RTL generation and verification.

Kernellum Compiler v0.1

A small but complete research prototype: train and quantize a workload, search accelerator parallelism, emit SystemVerilog and verification collateral, reproduce outputs in RTL simulation, and synthesize the design in CI.

96.44%INT8 held-out accuracy
450/450cycle-model exact matches
32/32RTL golden cases passed
7,977ECP5-mapped LUT4 primitives*
model → INT8 quantization → architecture search → RTL + memories → cycle model → RTL simulation → ECP5-family synthesis

*Family-mapped Yosys synthesis, not place-and-route utilization. The current 6.8 µs latency is modeled from 680 cycles at an assumed 100 MHz; physical timing and power are not yet claimed.
