What sets Kernellum apart

Hardware design becomes an evidence-producing loop.

  • Translate workload shapes, deployment constraints, and target characteristics into architecture-level objectives.

  • Explore parameterized accelerator candidates under explicit constraints rather than hand-picking one design.

  • Produce synthesizable implementations and test functional behavior before physical evaluation.

  • Use synthesis and routed timing to update the model, acquire new candidates, and repeat.
