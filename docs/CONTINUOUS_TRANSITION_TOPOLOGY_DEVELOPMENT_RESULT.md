# Continuous transition-topology development result

## Verdict

**KILL — the continuous transition-topology representation does not clear its family-transfer precision gate.**

This is an opened-cohort leave-one-family-out development result, not prospective evidence.

## Exact result

The audit combined the already-opened 16-path and 24-path continuation archives: 40 paths across five truth families. Both models used the same clipped far-shell gain target, standardization, ridge penalty (lambda 10), leave-one-family-out folds, and threshold grid.

No full-topology threshold with at least eight routes reached the required 0.75 precision. At thresholds 0.20, 0.25, and 0.30, the topology model was also dominated by a static operating point with route count within two.

## SCIENCE ledger

The continuation curves contain some predictive signal: every tested topology threshold improved aggregate far-shell loss and removed two opened-cohort catastrophes. But the full curve did not supply reliable incremental family-transfer discrimination. Its precision remained below 0.75, while the topology-free disagreement/residual control reached 0.75 precision with comparable coverage and slightly better aggregate loss.

Therefore the prior binary selector's failure is not repaired by replacing its thresholds with this straightforward continuous curve embedding.

## CONSEQUENCE ledger

Do not spend a fresh cohort on this representation and do not deploy a continuation-topology router. Retain continuation traces as descriptive telemetry only.

The next positive-mechanism kernel should exploit the stronger surviving clue: static candidate/globalized disagreement plus residual compatibility. It must turn that clue into a robust action—not another self-certifying confidence envelope—such as a validation-aware aggregation rule that blends or selects fits and prospectively reduces catastrophic tails.

## Smallest supported claim

On the opened 40-path archive, no tested ten-coordinate transition-topology ridge operating point met the predeclared precision, tail, and matched-static-control gate under leave-one-truth-family-out evaluation.

## Limitations

- All data were previously opened; this is a development falsification only.
- The result kills this representation and linear ridge readout, not continuation or topology methods generally.
- Thresholds came from a declared finite development grid; no external cohort was opened.
- The static ablation signal is not yet prospectively validated and is not deployment-ready.
