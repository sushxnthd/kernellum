Kernellum Research launches.

An independent research initiative exploring AI-native hardware-software co-design and efficient computing.

First release

The first public system is Kernellum Compiler v0.1, a reproducible model-to-RTL prototype. Its current evidence includes INT8 quantization, constraint-driven architecture search, exact 450-sample cycle-model agreement, 32 held-out RTL golden cases, and successful generic Yosys synthesis.

The next milestone is v0.2: ONNX graph lowering, a hardware IR and named FPGA-family mapping, followed by physical board validation.
