17 September 2026 / Launch note

Kernellum Research launches.

An independent research initiative exploring whether AI models and deployment constraints can help specify the machines that run them.

Explore the compiler →Build log
01 / First public system

A small, complete model-to-RTL path.

Kernellum Compiler v0.1 trains and quantizes a compact neural workload, searches parallel accelerator configurations, emits SystemVerilog and verifies the generated implementation.

The launch record includes 96.44% held-out INT8 accuracy, 450 of 450 exact cycle-model cases and 32 of 32 RTL golden-vector cases.

02 / Progress since launch

Physical feedback now changes the design.

v0.2 alpha adds a narrow ONNX front end and explicit hardware IR. A later ULX3S-85F place-and-route sweep selects the 2-lane design for the 25 MHz target and produces a reference bitstream in CI.

Physical-board correctness, latency, power and energy remain the next evidence threshold.
