17 September 2026 / launch note

Kernellum Research launches.

An independent research initiative exploring whether AI models and deployment constraints can help specify the machines that run them.

Explore the compiler →Build log
01 / First public system

A small, complete model-to-RTL path.

Kernellum Compiler v0.1 trains and quantizes a compact neural workload, searches parallel accelerator configurations under a modeled latency constraint, emits SystemVerilog and verifies the generated implementation.

The public record includes 96.44% held-out INT8 accuracy, 450/450 exact cycle-model agreement, 32/32 RTL golden-vector cases, and ECP5 family-mapped synthesis. Physical FPGA timing, board latency and power remain unclaimed.

02 / Next threshold

Physical evidence.

v0.2α adds a narrow ONNX front-end and explicit hardware IR. The next evidence threshold is a named physical ECP5 board, place-and-route, timing closure and measured latency/power.
