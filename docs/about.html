01 / Founder note

Why I built this.

Most AI work I encountered treated hardware as a fixed substrate. Pick a device, then optimize the model around it. Kernellum starts from the opposite direction.

If the workload and deployment constraints are known, can the compiler search the compute architecture too? The first prototypes are intentionally narrow because I would rather make one model-to-RTL path fully inspectable than claim support for everything.

Sushanth Dasari · 2026
02 / Operating principles

Constraints on the research itself.

LAB / 001
01

Claims follow artifacts.

Code, vectors, simulation, synthesis and measured hardware outrank adjectives.

02

Narrow is acceptable.

Unsupported structures are rejected explicitly. Scope grows when the implementation does.

03

Research should rerun.

The repository carries source, generated artifacts, tests and CI rather than screenshots alone.

KERNELLUM
03 / The name

Architecture before branding.

The name comes from an old architectural word associated with a battlement or pinnacle. The connection was accidental, but it fits: structure, architecture and building from the layer beneath.

04 / Position

Independent by design.

Kernellum is an independent research initiative. It is not presented as a fabricated semiconductor company. The ambition can be large without pretending the implementation is further along than it is.

That distinction matters because the lab's credibility should come from accumulating technical evidence—not from the title attached to it.
