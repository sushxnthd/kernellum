01 / Founder note

Why I built this.

Most AI work treats hardware as a fixed substrate. Pick a device, then optimize the model around it. Kernellum starts from the opposite direction.

If the workload and deployment constraints are known, can the compiler search the compute architecture too? The first prototypes are intentionally narrow. A complete and inspectable path matters more than a broad claim.

01 / Evidence

Claims follow artifacts.

Code, vectors, simulation, synthesis and measured hardware outrank adjectives.

02 / Scope

Narrow is acceptable.

Unsupported structures are rejected explicitly. Scope grows when the implementation does.

03 / Reproducibility

Research should rerun.

The repository carries source, generated artifacts, tests and CI rather than screenshots alone.

03 / Position

Build the evidence before the mythology.

Kernellum is building toward a compiler and IP platform for workload-specific acceleration.

It is not presented as a fabricated semiconductor company, and it does not claim hardware milestones that have not been earned. The thesis is larger than the current prototype. Credibility comes from closing that gap one inspectable threshold at a time.
