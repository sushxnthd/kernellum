About Kernellum

A small lab for a large systems question.

Can AI models and deployment constraints become inputs to the hardware-design process itself?

Position

Kernellum is an independent research initiative. It is not presented as a fabricated semiconductor company, and physical-hardware claims are only made when measured.

Founder note

Why I built this.

Most AI work I encountered treated hardware as a fixed substrate: pick a device, then optimize the model around it. Kernellum starts from the opposite direction. If we know the workload and the constraints, can we search for the compute architecture too?

The first prototypes are intentionally narrow. I would rather make one model-to-RTL path fully inspectable than claim support for everything. The long-term goal is much broader: a compiler that can reason jointly about model structure, precision, memory, parallelism and hardware cost.

Sushanth Dasari · 2026
Operating principles

How the lab works.

These are design constraints for the research itself, not marketing copy.

01 / Evidence

Claims follow artifacts.

Code, vectors, simulation logs, synthesis results and measured hardware are stronger than adjectives.

02 / Boundaries

Narrow is acceptable.

Unsupported graph structures are rejected explicitly. Scope expands when the implementation does.

03 / Reproducibility

Research should rerun.

The public repository carries source, generated artifacts, tests and CI rather than only screenshots of results.

Name

Kernellum.

The name comes from an old architectural word associated with a battlement or pinnacle. That accidental connection fits the lab: architecture, structure, and building from the layer beneath.
