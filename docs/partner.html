Evaluate the evidence, not the adjectives.

KRN / VC

The current proof is a reproducible model-to-RTL-to-reference-P&R loop: the ULX3S-85F timing sweep changes the architecture choice and produces a reference bitstream in CI. A pinned third-party ONNX workload has completed the clean-room L5 path, and an integrity-checked KRN-HW-001 handoff kit is ready for physical-board correctness, latency, power and energy evidence. A genuine outside design-partner workload remains a separate milestone.
