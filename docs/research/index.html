Research Result

K1 showed that analytical architecture rankings can survive final-route correction: 9/9 frozen workloads retained useful ranking structure, with mean Spearman ρ = 0.944.

Then we closed the loop.

With the same physical-feedback budget, active search improved the routed optimum on 12/12 Transformer GEMMs, while the random control improved 0/12.

Those who move quickest to adopt KERNELLUM's predictive capability will not just anticipate the future. They will define it.

Measured Breakthroughs

Results that survive held-out routing

    K1 routed search

    9/9 routes completed; mean ranking ρ = 0.944; the analytically selected winner had 0.583% mean final-routed regret.

    Closed-loop improvement

    Active search improved 12/12 workloads; equal-budget random search improved 0/12. Final routed-model latency was 16.74 ms vs 19.44 ms.

    SIMILARITY timing law
